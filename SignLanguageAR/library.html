How gesture references and training will work

  1. Verify the sign first. Have a qualified FSL signer, teacher, or interpreter confirm the sign form and note regional or contextual variants.
  2. Add a reviewed visual reference. Each entry currently has no verified 2D pose, video, or landmark sample. Avoid using a generated illustration as authoritative instruction.
  3. Collect varied, consented examples. Use the private local training toolkit with multiple signers; a single recording is not a reliable standard.
  4. Evaluate before recognition. Test on signers excluded from training and keep the sign marked pending until the results and reference are reviewed.

The existing collector is intentionally local and is not published as part of this public library. See the training toolkit guide to run it privately from your computer.

SignAR is an experimental learning prototype—not a substitute for FSL instruction or an interpreter.
